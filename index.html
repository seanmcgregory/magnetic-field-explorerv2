Model notes: These are qualitative 2-D teaching visualizations, not laboratory-accurate 3-D measurements. Magnets use dipole/virtual-pole approximations; the solenoid uses an idealized finite coil represented by a stack of dipoles. The horseshoe uses a simplified pole model. Solenoid field lines are schematic closed return paths; the probe field is a separate qualitative finite-coil approximation. Field lines are streamlines of the calculated field.
