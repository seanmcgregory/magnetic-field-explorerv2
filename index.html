Student investigation: Select each scenario on your worksheet. First hide field lines and predict the pattern. Turn them on to test your prediction. Trace several lines, include arrows, and label the poles. Compare the two-magnet arrangements. For solenoids, identify the nearly uniform interior field and the returning exterior field. For Earth's field, distinguish geographic north from the magnetic pole that attracts the north-seeking end of a compass.

Model notes: These are qualitative 2-D teaching visualizations, not laboratory-accurate 3-D measurements. Magnets use dipole/virtual-pole approximations; the solenoid uses an idealized finite coil represented by a stack of dipoles. The horseshoe uses a simplified pole model. Solenoid field lines are schematic closed return paths; the probe field is a separate qualitative finite-coil approximation. Field lines are streamlines of the calculated field. The directional-symbol panel shows prescribed uniform fields.
